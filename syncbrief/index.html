What it does

From raw audio to a brief you can act on.

🎙️

Record anywhere

One tap on iPhone, Mac or Apple Watch — plus a Control Center button and a watch complication. Meetings keep recording with the phone locked.

💻

Both sides of online calls

On the Mac, SyncBrief captures your mic and what the Mac plays — so Zoom, Meet, Teams and WhatsApp Desktop calls are recorded whole. It can even notice a meeting starting and offer to record it.

🧠

Summaries with receipts

A structured brief per meeting: timeline of topics, decisions, action items with owners, open questions — every timestamp taken from the transcript, never invented, and validated against the recording.

🗣️

Who said what

Speaker labels on every line — from your transcription provider, or identified on-device when the provider can't tell voices apart. Rename S1 to a real name once, it applies everywhere.

🗺️

Mind map

Each meeting drawn as a map — topics around the center, decisions and action items on their branches. Tap any node to jump the audio to that moment.

🔎

A library you can search

Everything lives in an encrypted database on your device with instant full-text search across transcripts, titles, speakers and summaries. Export to Markdown, PDF, text or JSON.

Audio gets in from everywhere

Import is free and touches no provider — transcription runs only when you say so.

📱

iPhone

In-app recorder, the share sheet from any app (including phone-call recordings made with iOS's built-in call recording), the Files app, and USB voice recorders via adapter.

🖥️

Mac

Drag & drop, a watched folder that imports anything dropped into it, and automatic detection of USB voice recorders — plug one in and SyncBrief offers what's new on it.

⌚

Apple Watch

Record on your wrist; the file lands in your iPhone library by itself over Watch Connectivity.

Simple pricing

Every feature is in the free tier. Pro is a subscription, and every plan starts with a 14-day free trial — no charge until it ends, cancel anytime.

Free

Pro · Monthly

Pro · Quarterly

Everything in Pro, billed every three months. 14-day free trial.

Pro · Yearly

Everything in Pro at the best value, billed yearly. 14-day free trial.

Prices are shown in the App Store in your local currency. Cloud transcription and summarizing additionally use your own provider API key, billed by that provider — or run fully offline on-device at no extra cost. Manage or cancel anytime in your Apple subscription settings.
